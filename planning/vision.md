# Product Vision & Feature List

> This is the **entry point** to planning-worx. Fill it in, then run `/plan-vision`
> in `claude` to ingest it into the master contract. Be concrete; vagueness here
> becomes ambiguity the agent fills in for you later.

## Problem

What problem are we solving, and for whom? What's broken or missing today?

## Outcome

When this works, what is different in the world? Describe the end state, not the features.

## Target users

- Who specifically will use this?

## Measurable goals

How will we know it worked? Prefer numbers.

- e.g. "A new user can sign up and complete onboarding in under 2 minutes."

## Principles / constitution (optional)

Durable rules for the whole project, including what NOT to build.

- MUST: ...
- NEVER: ...

## Non-goals (optional)

Things we are explicitly **not** doing in this scope. The single best guard
against over-engineering — write these down.

Hard limits (tech, budget, timeline, compliance).

- ...
